## Accepted for v0.2.0

- These are documented limitations, carried over from the pre-M3 closure:
  - signals passed between v0.1.x and v0.2 through effects or hooks are not reactive;
  - RSC server components are not detected by the transform (`exclude`, `@noSignalTracking`, or `mode: "manual"` are the workarounds);
  - the T2 ordering guarantee holds against plugins without an `order`;
  - React Compiler's `oxc-transform-react` route and the Babel compiler route were verified only through an emulated plugin shape.
- The transform package requires Node `^22.18.0 || >=24.11.0` (Babel 8), which is narrower than the runtime's `>=22.0.0`.
- The packed runtime manifest includes `devDependencies`, `devEngines`, and scripts, as noted above.
- Lint warnings are pre-existing and non-blocking.
